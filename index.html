Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common student and educator queries.

Is EduBridge AI free for students?
Yes! All foundational learning modules, diagnostic quizzes, project lab guides, and local practice coaches are completely free and work directly in modern web browsers.
How does multilingual teaching work?
The platform supports seamless switching between English, Telugu, and Hindi interface translations, while our AI Tutor can explain deep technical topics in regional contexts with real-life analogies.
How is my student data stored?
In this standalone browser application, all non-sensitive study records, notes, and quiz milestones are safely saved directly in your browser's private localStorage. No secret credentials or passwords are ever stored insecurely.
